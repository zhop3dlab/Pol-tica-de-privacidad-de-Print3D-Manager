# Print3D Manager Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 25, 2026**

_This is a translation of the canonical Spanish version, [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](../../PRIVACY_POLICY.md). In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails._

This policy explains what data Print3D Manager ("the app") processes, for what purpose, on what legal basis, for how long it is kept, and what choices you have. It is written to describe exactly what the app does today, not future features.

## 1. Who processes the data

Print3D Manager is an independently developed app for managing a 3D-printing workshop (costs, printers, materials, projects, customers, quotes, and invoices).

Contact: **zhop3dlab@gmail.com**.

## 2. General principle: the app is local by default

All the information you enter (printers, materials, projects, customers, quotes, invoices, inventory) is stored **on your own device** by default. You don't need to create an account or have an internet connection to use the app with all of its core features.

None of your business data is sent to our servers or to third parties unless you have the Business plan and voluntarily enable cloud sync (see section 3), or while an ad is being shown (see section 6).

## 3. Account and cloud sync (Business plan, optional)

If you have the Business plan and decide to create an account (with email and password, or with your Google account) to also store your data in the cloud and use it on more than one device:

- **Account data**: email address and, if you use Google, the name and profile picture associated with that Google account.
- **Business data**: if you enable sync, a copy of your projects, customers, printers, materials, quotes, and invoices is stored in a cloud database (Google Firebase/Firestore), tied only to your account and your workspace. No one outside your account can access this data.
- You can stop using the cloud at any time and go back to the app working purely locally.
- You can **delete your account and all associated cloud data** at any time from within the app, under **More → Account → Delete account and data**. This action is permanent and immediate.

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) as the infrastructure provider for the account and the cloud. Google acts as a data processor for this data under its own terms: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>.

**Legal basis**: we process this data because it is necessary to provide the service you have contracted (performance of a contract/use of the Business plan) and, for the contact email, with your consent when creating the account.

**Retention**: for as long as you keep your account active. If you delete it (More → Account → Delete account and data), the cloud data is deleted immediately. If you never create an account, no cloud data is ever generated.

**International transfers**: Google Firebase's infrastructure may process and store data in data centers outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Google provides GDPR safeguards (standard contractual clauses) for these transfers — more detail at the link above.

## 4. If you use the app to manage your own customers' data

If you store your customers' data in the app (name, address, tax ID, email...) for quotes and invoices, **you are the data controller for that data** in relation to your own customers — the same as if you kept it in a spreadsheet or invoicing program. Print3D Manager is only the tool you use to store it (and, if you have the Business plan with sync enabled, the cloud data processor).

This means it's your responsibility to have an adequate legal basis for processing your customers' data (usually your commercial/contractual relationship with them) and to handle any access, rectification, or deletion requests they make directly to you.

## 5. Data that never leaves your device

- **G-code files**: if you use the G-code analyzer, the file is read and analyzed entirely on your device. The file's content is never uploaded to the internet or shared with anyone; only you see the extracted data (estimated time, grams, temperatures).
- **Local backups**: exporting/importing your data as a JSON file (Pro and Business plans) is a fully local operation — the file is saved wherever you choose (for example, by sharing it yourself via email or saving it to your own storage).
- **Quote and invoice PDFs**: generated on your device. Sharing or downloading them is your own explicit action; the app doesn't send them to any server of ours.

## 6. Advertising

The app shows ads via **Google AdMob** to users on the free plan. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers from your device to show ads (personalized or not, depending on your operating system's privacy settings) and measure their performance. This processing is carried out by Google under its own privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and its AdMob-specific policy: <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>.

**Legal basis**: consent (managed through your device's/Google's privacy settings) and legitimate interest in funding the free app through advertising.

Users on paid plans (Pro and Business) don't see ads.

## 7. What we DON'T do

- We don't sell your data to anyone.
- We don't access the content of your projects, customers, or invoices unless you have the Business plan and expressly enable cloud sync — and even then, we don't manually review it.
- We don't use your business data to train artificial intelligence models.
- We don't request camera, contacts, location, or microphone permissions — the app doesn't need them and doesn't ask for them.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict processing of, object to, and request portability of your data. In practice, from within the app you can:

- **See** how much data you have stored locally and in the cloud (More → Account).
- **Download** a copy of your data as a JSON file (Pro and Business plans, More → Data).
- **Delete** your account and all associated cloud data from within the app (More → Account → Delete account and data).
- **Uninstall the app** at any time, which removes all data stored locally on your device.

If you need help with any of these rights, or have any questions about this policy, contact **zhop3dlab@gmail.com**.

If you believe your request has not been handled properly, you have the right to file a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country (in Spain, the **Agencia Española de Protección de Datos**, <https://www.aepd.es>).

## 9. Minors

The app is not directed at minors, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top of the document will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change implies acceptance of the updated policy.
