Print3D Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

_This is a translation of the canonical Spanish version, PRIVACY_POLICY.md. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails._

This policy explains what data Print3D Manager ("the app") processes, for what purpose, on what legal basis, for how long it is kept, and what choices you have. It is written to describe exactly what the app does today, not future features.

1. Who processes the data

Print3D Manager is an independently developed app for managing a 3D-printing workshop (costs, printers, materials, projects, customers, quotes, and invoices).

Contact: zhop3dlab@gmail.com.

2. General principle: the app is local

All the information you enter (printers, materials, projects, customers, quotes, invoices, inventory) is stored only on your own device. You don't need to create any account or have an internet connection to use the app.

No business data is ever sent to our servers or to third parties.

3. If you use the app to manage your own customers' data

If you store your customers' data in the app (name, address, tax ID, email...) for quotes and invoices, you are the data controller for that data in relation to your own customers — the same as if you kept it in a spreadsheet or invoicing program. Print3D Manager is only the tool you use to store it, always on your own device.

This means it's your responsibility to have an adequate legal basis for processing your customers' data (usually your commercial/contractual relationship with them) and to handle any access, rectification, or deletion requests they make directly to you.

4. Data that never leaves your device

  • G-code files: if you use the G-code analyzer, the file is read and analyzed entirely on your device. Its content is never uploaded or shared with anyone.
  • Local backups: exporting/importing your data as a JSON file is a fully local operation.
  • Quote and invoice PDFs: generated on your device. Sharing or downloading them is your own explicit action.
  • Delivery reminders: a project's due-date alert is a notification scheduled entirely by your device's own operating system; it never goes through any server of ours.

5. What we DON'T do

  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't access the content of your projects, customers, or invoices — we don't even manually review it.
  • We don't use your business data to train artificial intelligence models.
  • We don't request camera, contacts, location, or microphone permissions — the app doesn't need them and doesn't ask for them.
  • We never send or store any of your business data on any server.
  • We don't show ads or use any advertising identifier — the app is free and has no active paid feature right now.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict processing of, object to, and request portability of your data. Since all your data lives only on your device, you can exercise these rights yourself at any time:

  • Download a copy of your data as a JSON file (More → Data).
  • Uninstall the app at any time, which removes all data stored on your device.

If you need help with any of these rights, or have any questions about this policy, contact zhop3dlab@gmail.com.

If you believe your request has not been handled properly, you have the right to file a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, https://www.aepd.es).

7. Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top of the document will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change implies acceptance of the updated policy.
